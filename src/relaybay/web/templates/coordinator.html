One coordinator for all projects: a run of your own claude without a card, in a folder of its own outside any git repository. It plans and drives the tasks on this board through relaybay's tools; it never approves and never answers your questions for you. Start makes it ready without a turn and without tokens; your text below, or the first wake by events on the board, is its first turn. Between wakes no process runs.

Runs need a server started by relaybay serve or the program.
