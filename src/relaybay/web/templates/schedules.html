A clock in the program, in this machine's local time ({{ timezone }}). A coordinator turn is a line in the coordinator's next wake (skipped while no coordinator run is open). A read-only check is a one-shot claude -p without a session, at most 8 steps, under a check profile, in the card's worktree or the project's folder; it waits for a slot like any run, and its answer becomes a report on the card, or an Inbox line without one. A reminder is a tray notification and an Inbox line, no model. A time missed by less than an hour (the program closed, the machine asleep) runs once when it is back; an older one is logged as missed. One run of a schedule at a time.
